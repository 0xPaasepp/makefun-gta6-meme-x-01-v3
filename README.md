A GTA VI boarding pass meme: after 13 years at the gate, flight VI-2026 finally boards for Vice City on May 26, 2026. Every field on the pass is a joke — gate 13, seat 13A, 214 GB of baggage, economy paid in youth — and my AI agent paid for the ticket on make.fun while I waited. Your agent flies first. You just pack.

The image is built deterministically by `meme.py` (Python + Pillow, real Liberation type, no randomness or network) into `meme.png` (1080x1080). The post caption is in `CAPTION.txt`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 meme.py
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
